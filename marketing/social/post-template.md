# Post template

The structure and the checklist. Use this so writing a post doesn't mean rereading the whole
strategy each time.

---

## Structure

Four parts, in this order. Most posts should be shorter than this scaffold implies.

**1. Hook — the problem, in the reader's words.**
Not what the product does. What's annoying. "Most clippers turn a Reddit thread into
unreadable prose" beats "Discerned features advanced structure preservation."

**2. What it does about that.** One or two sentences. Concrete.

**3. The thing that's actually unusual.** Pick one — don't list features. For Track B this is
almost always layout-preserving capture (C-02, C-03). For Track A it's that the evaluation is
yours, signed by your key (C-11).

**4. Call to action.** A link, or the standard line: "View more discerns at discerned.online".

**Optional fifth: what it doesn't do.** Naming a limitation up front buys more credibility than
another feature, especially on Hacker News and in PKM communities. "Chrome only for now,
Firefox planned" costs nothing and pre-empts the first reply.

## Pre-post checklist

Run this every time. It takes under a minute.

- [ ] **Every product claim traces to an ID in `../copy/claims.md`.** If you wrote something
      new, add the row with its proof first.
- [ ] **Read the Constraints section of `claims.md`**, not just the claim rows — particularly
      if the post mentions both privacy and ownership, which is where the "encrypted" misread
      forms.
- [ ] **Banned-phrase pass** against `../copy/banned-phrases.md`.
- [ ] **Right track for the venue.** Track B posts don't lead with Nostr. Track A posts don't
      undersell the capture.
- [ ] **Planned features labelled planned** — tipping, voting, Firefox, mobile. Encryption and
      publish-retry aren't planned enough to mention.
- [ ] **"Discerns", not "discernments". "Unless you cast it", not "until".**
- [ ] **The user is the subject.** Not "it rates pages" — "you rate what you clip".
- [ ] **Channel rules re-read today.** Not from memory, not from this file — rules change, and
      the cost of getting this wrong is a removal or a shadowban rather than a bad post.
- [ ] **Not cross-posting the same body simultaneously** anywhere, especially Reddit.

---

## Length

Shorter than feels right. A post that reads as an announcement gets skipped; one that reads as
a person mentioning a thing they made gets read.

If you're over 150 words outside a Show HN comment or a long-form Nostr note, cut.

---

## Replying

Most of the value in B3 and B4 comes from replies, not posts. When answering someone's
question, the same rules apply — but lead with the answer to what they asked, and mention
Discerned only if it genuinely fits. A reply that solves their problem without mentioning it
is still worth writing.

If Discerned doesn't actually fit their case, say so. That's the reputation the two anti-drive-by
channels are built on.
