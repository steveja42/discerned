### 1. The Three Axes of Worth

By splitting these up, you allow for nuanced ratings (e.g., something can be *Evil* but *Incredibly Interesting*).

- **Axis 1: Interest Level (The "Hook")**

  - *Default:* **Interesting**

  - *Range:* Boring → Noteworthy → Mind-blowing.

  - *Marketing name:* **The Pulse.** It measures the "energy" of the content.

- **Axis 2: Ethics Level (The "Compass")**

  - *Default:* **Good**

  - *Range:* Malicious/Bad → Neutral → Virtuous/Good.

  - *Marketing name:* **The Valence.** This is the "Moral North" of the site or clip.

- **Axis 3: Category (The "Bucket")**

  - *Default:* **Main**

  - *Range:* User-defined (e.g., Tech, Politics, Hobbies).

  - *Marketing name:* **The Frequency.** Where the signal is being broadcast.

## Onboarding Text (The First 3 Sentences)

When a user installs the extension for the first time, these three screens (or sentences) will define the experience:

### Screen 1: The Action

> **"Stop scrolling and start casting: Assign a 'Worth'—Good, Bad, or Interesting—to any website or clip with a single click."**

### Screen 2: The Protocol

> **"Your voice, decentralized: Every value you assign is broadcast as a signed note over Nostr, building a global map of the web's best content."**

### Screen 3: The Library

> **"Build your Signal Log: Instantly clip and save noteworthy moments to your personal library, creating a permanent archive of everything worth remembering."**


### **Marketing the Library Feature**

You don’t just want a "Folder"; you want a library that feels as cool as the "Cast."

- **Call it the "Signal Log" or "WorthVault":** \* **Signal Log:** Fits the "Broadcasting/Tower" theme. It sounds like a captain's log of everything noteworthy you've encountered.

  - **WorthVault:** Sounds secure and high-value, emphasizing that these clippings are the "gold" you've found on the web.

- **Use the "Clipped" terminology:** Instead of "Save to Library," use **"Archive the Signal"** or **"Capture the Cast."**

### 3. How to Display it on the Website

Since you mentioned a website that displays these clips:

- **The "Station" View:** Each user could have their own "Broadcasting Station." This is their profile where all their "WorthCasts" (ratings) and "Clippings" (saved content) are displayed.

- **The "Replay" Room:** A specific section for clips where users can watch the most "WorthCasted" (highly rated) videos from the community.

### 4. Digital Marketing Tips for Your Library

- **The "Nostr" Advantage:** Since your library is connected to Nostr, tell users: *"Your library isn't trapped in a silo. It’s your data, signed by your key."* This is a huge selling point for privacy-conscious users.

- **Searchable "Signal Tags":** Let users search their library by the "Worth" they assigned. (e.g., "Show me all my *Interesting* clips from last month.")

- **The "Highlight Reel":** Automatically generate a "Weekly WorthCast" for users—a summary of the best things they clipped that week to share with their Nostr followers.



## Marketing


#### **A. Lean into "Signal vs. Noise"**

In the Nostr and Web3 world, "Signal vs. Noise" is a major pain point. Position WorthCast as the **Filter**.

- **Tagline Idea:** *"The Global Signal for What Matters."*

- ***Tagline Idea:** "Cast your signal. Set the moral compass of the web." *

- **Marketing Angle:** "Stop scrolling the noise. Tune into the WorthCast."

#### **B. Gamify the "Casting"**

Since it’s an extension, the friction to use it must be zero.

- **The "One-Click Cast":** Use a simple UI in the browser—maybe a small slider or three simple icons (Up/Down/Interesting).

- **User "Rep":** If someone "Casts" a site as "Good" and 1,000 other people agree later, give that user a "Discovery Score."

#### **C. The "Proof of Worth"**

Because you are using Nostr, emphasize that these aren't just anonymous reviews like Yelp—they are **signed** by public keys.

- **Marketing Angle:** "Reviews you can trust, signed by the people you follow."

- **Niche Appeal:** Market to the "curators." There are people who love being the first to find a "hidden gem" clip or site. WorthCast is their megaphone.

#### **D. Visual Consistency for "Clips"**

Since you are also displaying clips on a website, create a "WorthCast Stamp" (a small watermark or overlay).

- When a clip is shared, it should have a small "Worth: Interesting" or "Worth: 8.5" visual tag. This turns every shared clip into an advertisement for your platform.


### Messaging Strategy (The "Elevator Pitch")

> **"WorthCast is a decentralized layer for the internet's value. Using a simple browser extension, you can 'cast' your judgment—good, bad, or just plain interesting—on any website or clip. Your values are broadcast over Nostr, creating a global, censorship-resistant map of what’s actually worth our time."**

### **Other Marketing Tips for your Brand:**

**1. The "Signal vs. Noise" Concept** The internet is full of "noise" (junk content). Your marketing should focus on the idea that **WorthCast** provides the "signal" (the valuable stuff).

- **Visual idea:** Show a blurry, static-filled screen of websites, and then a clean, bright "Beacon" beam cutting through it to show a high-value clip.

**2. Focus on "Proof of Human"** Because you are using Nostr, these values are signed by real people.

- **Marketing Angle:** "Algorithms decide what you see. WorthCast lets *people* decide what it's worth."

**3. The "Worth Score" Visual** Since you are doing clips, create a very recognizable "WorthCast Badge." It could be a simple circle with the beacon icon and a number or a color. When people see that badge on a video clip, they should instantly know, "Oh, the community says this is Worthy."

**4. Use the "Ascribe" definition** Since we started this conversation with the word **Ascribe** (giving value to something), you can use that in your high-end marketing:

- *"Don't just browse the web. Ascribe it. WorthCast."*
