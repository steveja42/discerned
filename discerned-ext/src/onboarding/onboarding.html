📡 Discerned

Value attribution for the Open Social Web

Pin Discerned to your toolbar

1

Click the Extensions puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's top-right toolbar.

2

Find Discerned in the list.

3

Click the pin icon 📌 next to it so the 📡 icon stays visible in your toolbar.

4

Browse to any page, then right-click or click the 📡 icon to capture and evaluate content.

If you have a Nostr signing extension (Alby, nos2x) installed, Discerned will detect it automatically when you browse. You can connect a Nostr identity anytime from within the capture overlay.
