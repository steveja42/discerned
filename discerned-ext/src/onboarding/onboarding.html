Clip it, rate it, keep it. Build your own library of what's worth reading.

Discerned helps you build your own trusted library of high-quality information.

Pin Discerned to your toolbar

1

Click the Extensions puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's top-right toolbar.

2

Find Discerned in the list.

3

Click the pin icon 📌 next to it so the Discerned beacon stays visible in your toolbar.

4

Browse to any page, then right-click or click the Discerned beacon to clip and rate what you're reading.

Your clips stay on this device until you choose to publish. Publishing uses Nostr — an open social network where you own your identity and your posts.
