Discerned

Clip it, rate it, keep it, share it. Build your own library of high-quality information.

Pin Discerned to your toolbar

1

Click the Extensions puzzle-piece icon in Chrome's top-right toolbar.

2

Find the Discerned beacon in the list.

3

Click the pin icon 📌 next to it so the Discerned beacon stays visible in your toolbar.

4

Browse to any page, then right-click or click the Discerned beacon to clip and rate the page content.

Take your clips beyond this device

You can share your clips with the world by publishing to Nostr — an open social network where you own your identity, posts and connections. Find out more →

Save images with your clips

Discerned can store a copy of each image inside the clip*, so it still looks right if the original page changes or disappears. Without this, clips link back to the pictures on the original site, which can break over time.

* Your private clips only. Anything you publish always links to images where they already live — stored copies are far too large to publish.
